# one-sentence-journey

A complete story about a journey, told in exactly one sentence, for the [One Sentence, One Journey](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gudril-cool-one-sentence-one-journey-410686ca) bounty (make.fun `u-gudril-cool-one-sentence-one-journey-410686ca`).

## The sentence

> He walked the last twelve kilometres to the monastery in the rain because his grandmother had promised the icon would heal his sister, and when the monks finally opened the gate at dawn he understood the walk itself had been the prayer, not the icon.

One sentence, 250 characters. A pilgrimage on foot, the gate opening at dawn, and the turn: the walk itself was the prayer, not the icon.
